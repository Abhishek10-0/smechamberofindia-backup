Visit of

Consul General People's Republic of China in Mumbai

to the Head office of SME Chamber of India, World SME Trade Centre and Maharashtra Industry Development Association

08th November 2022 | Mumbai

His Excellency, Mr. KONG Xianhua, Consul General of The People’s Republic of China in Mumbai, visited the Head office of SME CHAMBER OF INDIA and Maharashtra Industry Development Association, as well as World SME Trade Centre and SME Industry Parks of India on 8th November 2022, along with Mr. Liu Mingming, Commercial Counselor of the Consulate.

Mr. Chandrakant Salunkhe - Founder and President, SME Chamber of India and Maharashtra Industry Development Association welcomed the Consul General and his team. The Consul General requested for the support and assistance from SME Chamber of India and World SME Trade Centre for visiting Chinese Companies, who are looking for various business opportunities in Maharashtra and Karnataka related to exports, imports, joint venture, technology transfer, marketing, distribution as well as strategic business partnership with SMEs and manufacturing industries for contract manufacturing tie-ups and readymade industrial premises or working manufacturing units and industrial land in Maharashtra.

The Consul General has also sought support of Mr. Salunkhe for resolving the issues and grievances of Chinese companies, those who have invested in Maharashtra and operating their businesses and also to take up to the appropriate government authorities for final redressal. The Consul General and Mr. Salunkhe have agreed to organize various business events and activities on the platform of India-China business Council to provide a platform to Indian and Chinese SMEs and Companies to explore emerging business opportunities and strategic business partnership as well as to organize various business meetings and events for visiting Government officials, ministers and business delegations to Mumbai and other parts of Maharashtra and Karnataka.

SME Chamber of India is a national level organisation, supporting Indian and foreign SMEs and enterprises for the last 29 years. The Chamber has been supporting SMEs for the business growth, expansion and diversification, exploring emerging businesses opportunities in exports, investment, joint ventures, technology transfers, contract manufacturing, collaborations, marketing and business advisory services to the companies, who are looking to set up business or manufacturing units, looking for industrial land or readymade industrial premises or strategic business partners in Maharashtra and other parts of India. The Chamber is associated with the Government of Maharashtra and Central Government and providing government services to resolve various issues and grievances of Indian and foreign SMEs and manufacturing industries.

The Chamber has initiated "World SME Trade Centre" at Mumbai to provide a platform for visiting delegations to enhance business co-operation with local SMEs as well as B2B / B2C meetings, business conferences and branding of various products and services in the State and other parts of India.
